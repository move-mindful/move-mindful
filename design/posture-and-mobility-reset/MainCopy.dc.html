Modern life makes your body stiff. The right movement can help you feel like yourself again.

Long periods of sitting and screen time can leave your body feeling stiff, achy, and restricted. This 5-Day Reset is designed to counteract common sedentary patterns and help your body move more freely. Each routine has a specific focus, progressing from targeted mobility and strength to coordinated full-body movement.

Your 5-Day Posture & Mobility Reset

No equipment needed — just a folded blanket or towel.

Day 1: Posture Foundations

Gentle hip and neck movements to release tension and improve mobility, then we'll work to activate and strengthen the muscles along the back of the body that support a more upright, balanced posture.

12 min

Day 2: Core & Hip Support

Create space through the hips while strengthening the core and improving control around the pelvis, building a more stable foundation for better posture.

12 min

Day 3: Reversing Rounded Shoulders

Open the chest and improve shoulder mobility with targeted movements that help counteract slouching and make a more upright posture feel natural.

11 min

Day 4: Move Your Spine in all Directions

Move your spine through flexion, extension, side bending, and rotation to improve mobility, ease stiffness, and help your entire back feel less restricted.

11 min

Day 5: Full Body Freedom

Bring everything together with dynamic movements that mobilize the shoulders, move the spine, open the hips, and help your entire body move with greater freedom and ease.

11 min

What We’ll Work On Together:

Forward head posture and “tech neck”

Mobilize the neck and upper back while strengthening the deep muscles that help support your head in better alignment.

Rounded shoulders and a tight chest

Open the chest and front of the shoulders while strengthening the muscles that support and stabilize your shoulder blades.

A stiff or rounded upper back

Restore movement through the thoracic spine so standing taller, reaching, and rotating feel easier.

Limited shoulder and overhead mobility

Improve shoulder and shoulder-blade mobility so reaching overhead feels smoother and less restricted.

Weak postural and core muscles

Build strength and control through the core, back, and stabilizing muscles that support better posture.

Tight hips and lower-back stiffness

Mobilize the hips and pelvis to help everyday movements like standing, walking, and bending feel less stiff.
