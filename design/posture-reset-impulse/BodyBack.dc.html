The problem

It’s not just age. It’s how you spend your day.

Your body adapts to the positions you spend the most time in. Hours of sitting, screens, and looking down slowly shape you forward, until rounded shoulders and a stiff upper back stop feeling like a slouch and start feeling like you.

Why what you’re doing isn’t working

You’ve been told “Just sit up straight.”
What’s really going on

Posture held by willpower lasts until you stop thinking about it. Your muscles need to be trained to support you.

You’ve been told “Just stretch it out.”
What’s really going on

Stretching can feel good, but without strength and control your body drifts back into the same shape.

You’ve been told “You need long workouts.”
What’s really going on

Short, targeted movement is easier to repeat, and repetition is what helps retrain a pattern.

Simply pulling your shoulders back won’t address the entire pattern. Your body needs movement where it’s stiff and strength where it’s weak.

The solution

Intelligent, mindful movement that works with your body

Short, follow-along routines combine mobility, strengthening, therapeutic techniques, simple props, and body awareness, so your body can let go of the shape modern life has pressed it into.
