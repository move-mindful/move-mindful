Sitting up straight It lasts until you stop thinking about it. The muscles that should hold you there haven’t been trained to.
Pulling your shoulders back It won’t address the entire pattern: a stiff upper back, a tight chest, and weak postural muscles.

Why trust this

Ayla has spent over a decade helping people move with more freedom and ease

Ayla Sarnoff is the founder of Move Mindful, creator of the Therapeutic Yoga Method, and owner of Clarity of Heart Yoga Studio in Sedona, Arizona.
For more than a decade, Ayla has helped thousands of people improve their posture, reclaim their strength, and move with greater freedom. 

After experiencing a femur fracture as a child and a severe ankle fracture as a teenager, Ayla spent years navigating restricted mobility and chronic pain. Yoga, mobility work, and mindful movement became her path to recovery—helping her restore lost ranges of motion, rebuild strength, and rediscover her body’s capacity to heal.
Her teaching style is practical, creative, and down-to-earth, combining therapeutic movement, yoga, and strength training principles to make movement both effective and accessible. What began as Ayla’s personal journey back to movement has evolved into a complete method designed to help others feel stronger, move more freely, and feel at home in their bodies.
